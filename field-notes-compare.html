Four ways to make the Field Notes demos readable.

The section shipping today renders each demo at a forced 1280px viewport and then shrinks it to 29%, so 15px body copy lands at 4.3px. Every layout below deletes that scaling. The demo gets its real width and runs its own responsive CSS, so nothing is reduced and nothing is cropped.

Held constant across all four

  • Type, palette, radii, matted plates and corner crop marks are the incumbent section's, unchanged.
  • Copy is identical in all four, so the comparison is about layout. Two card titles were moved to sentence case to match the third.
  • Demos mount only when the section is within 400px of the viewport. Today's version eagerly loads two iframes and two autoplay videos, roughly 8MB, whether or not anyone scrolls down.
  • Each demo has a visible, keyboard-reachable control that opens it at full size. Today the demos are interactive but nothing says so.
  • Reduced motion collapses every entrance and transition.
  • The section stays a light paper island, which is how it reads on the live site.

Still open

  • The Field Notes label above the heading is kept in all four because it is the section's name. The heading carries fine without it if you want it gone.
