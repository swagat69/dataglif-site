What we learn building datasets.

Manual + Semi-Automatic Tagging

Combining expert annotation with SAM models

We combine expert frame-level annotation with Meta's latest SAM models to speed up complex driving sequences without sacrificing accuracy. GPU-accelerated inference and temporal tracking help generate consistent masks across frames, while human annotators review and refine the results. This allows us to move faster while preserving the details that matter most such as lane boundaries, vehicle trajectories, occlusions, and merge behavior.

Custom Annotation Tooling

Extending CVAT for rotation-aware 3D annotation

Driver's-view footage constantly changes a vehicle's angle and perspective. CVAT's default cuboid controls do not represent those rotations naturally. We create our own rotation-aware cuboids making 3D alignment faster, more intuitive, and more consistent across frames.

Annotation Intelligence

Agent-assisted calibration for higher IAA

We combine expert review with advanced AI models to identify annotation disagreements, uncover unclear guidelines, and highlight where annotators may need additional support. Each review cycle helps us improve the rubric, provide targeted coaching, and increase consistency while keeping human judgment at the center of the process.

Document Intelligence

Layout is the context layer

OCR can find the words; structure reveals what they mean. Procurement packages, job postings, and legal documents encode context through sections, tables, lists, and hierarchy. We build and validate that structural layer first, giving downstream models a stronger foundation for extracting skills, clauses, dates, contract terms, and other critical information.
