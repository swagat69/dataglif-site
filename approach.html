Start With the Failure Mode.

Show us what the model is missing, where the data breaks down, or what quality must be proven. That becomes the brief for the right collection, annotation, enrichment, and review workflow.

Open a Data Brief Or send a note / hello@dataglif.com
01 / DATA
What are you working with?

Raw images, LiDAR & 3D point clouds, unstructured document scans, conversational audio, or pre-existing datasets requiring higher quality labels.

02 / GAP
What is failing or missing?

Edge-case classification errors, OCR transcription drift, bounding box misalignment, or low confidence in complex domain taxonomy.

03 / TARGET
What must production-ready mean?

>99.5% verified consensus accuracy, schema-compliant JSON/Parquet export, multi-tier QA audits, and delivery within your SLA.
