End-to-End Pipeline

Every project follows a structured workflow designed to deliver consistent quality at scale.

Stage One

Discovery & Requirements

We start by understanding your model architecture, target use cases, and existing data landscape. Together we define annotation schemas, edge cases, and success metrics.

Annotation Guidelines We draft schemas and labeling instructions with your team, then iterate based on real samples.
Timeline & Milestones Fixed scope with delivery checkpoints. Pilot batch in 5 business days.
Stage Two

Team Recruitment & Training

We assemble a team with the right domain expertise. For medical imaging, we recruit radiologists. For legal documents, we bring in paralegals. Every annotator passes qualification tests before production work.

Domain Specialists We match annotators to your content. Medical, legal, geospatial, multilingual.
Qualification Process Gold-standard test sets with known-correct answers. Minimum pass rate: 92%.
Stage Three

Annotation & Quality Control

Multi-pass workflows with automated validation and human review. Continuous monitoring of inter-annotator agreement and feedback loops to catch drift early.

Multi-Pass Review First-pass annotation, automated checks, second-pass validation, final QA spot-check.
Real-Time Dashboards Track progress, agreement scores, and quality metrics as work proceeds.
Stage Four

Delivery & Handoff

Final validation pass, format conversion to your pipeline spec, and delivery with comprehensive quality reports. NDA on request. All annotator data handled under strict confidentiality agreements.

Format Flexibility COCO, Pascal VOC, YOLO, custom JSON schemas. S3, GCS, or direct API.
Security & Compliance SOC 2 Type II in progress. NDA standard. Data wiped post-delivery on request.

Quality Standards

We measure what matters and build feedback loops into every stage of the workflow.

Inter-Annotator Agreement

Target IAA > 0.85 for classification tasks, IoU > 0.90 for segmentation. Continuous monitoring with automatic flagging of disagreement clusters.

Gold-Standard Validation

Every batch includes 8-12% known-correct samples. Annotators who fall below accuracy threshold are automatically routed to retraining or removed from the project.

Edge Case Coverage

We actively source rare classes, adversarial examples, and boundary conditions to prevent model blind spots.

Continuous Feedback

Weekly guideline updates based on new edge cases. Model-in-the-loop pipelines to catch annotation drift early.
