Annotation Quality

Why inter-annotator agreement is the only QA metric that matters

Accuracy against a gold set tells you how well annotators match one reviewer's judgement. Agreement across independent passes tells you whether your labelling guidelines are actually unambiguous — and that is what determines how a model behaves on data it has never seen.

Pipelines

Edge cases belong in your spec, not your backlog

Occlusion, truncation, and ambiguous class boundaries decide label consistency. Resolve them before the first batch, not after the audit.

Human in the Loop

Synthetic data scales volume. Humans still set the ceiling.

Generated data compounds the biases of the model that produced it. Human validation is what keeps a dataset anchored to the real distribution.
